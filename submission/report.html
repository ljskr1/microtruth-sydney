MicroTruth Sydney: Technical & Methodology Report

Team: Oneshot (team-29) Event: The Microburbs Hackathon 2026 Venue: UTS Business School, Level 2 Repository: github.com/ljskr1/microtruth-sydney

1. Executive Summary & Lead Insights

💡 The Clever Step (Judges' Primary Factor):
Most hackathon entries default to building an isolated web dashboard that asks users to re-type addresses. In real life, property buyers never use detached dashboards during search. MicroTruth operates in-situ where buyers already are: directly injecting certified municipal geospatial truth, underquote reality corridors, and inside-the-building transaction histories beside listings on Domain.com.au and Realestate.com.au.

In Sydney real estate, buyers and tenants face severe asymmetric information. Listing agents routinely underquote price guides by 15% to 25% to generate artificial bidding frenzy at auction, while concealing strata defects, past lease histories, and nearby public housing clusters from brochures. Buyers waste thousands of dollars on strata and building inspections for properties they cannot afford.

MicroTruth Sydney ends this asymmetry across all five competition tasks:

  • Task 1 (Showcase & Pitch): High-resolution Playwright headless capture + OpenScreen kinetic zooms composited into a strict 41.93s pitch video and a 90.8s Australian-narrated demo presentation.
  • Tasks 2 & 3 (Chrome Extension & AR Data): Manifest V3 browser extension featuring an in-page Underquote Reality Badge and slide-in drawer backed by a local SQLite WAL engine (<2ms response) querying 112,457 leases, 81,079 sales, and 213,239 municipal addresses.
  • Task 4 (Rental Pricing): GBDT pricing model reducing Median Dollar Error to $59.16/wk (beating the Microburbs $86.00 baseline by 31.2%) on 65,777 Sydney flats.
  • Task 5 (City-Wide Valuation): Repeat-sales hedonic valuation index valuing all 181,711 City of Sydney properties.

Task 4: Date-Split Validation & Training Methodology

Per the task contract: "Use earlier adverts to build your estimate. Check it against later adverts dated by 31 July 2026. Never use those later adverts while building or tuning your method."

  • Temporal Partitioning: We split the 112,457 historical leases chronologically:
    • Training Set: 103,919 leases dated on or before 31 December 2025.
    • Held-Out Test Set: 8,538 leases dated between 1 January 2026 and 31 July 2026. Zero leakage from future or scoring-period adverts.
  • Feature Engineering:
    • Spatial Target Encoding: Suburb and ABS Mesh Block median rents with empirical Bayes smoothing.
    • Property Attributes: Non-linear interactions between bedroom count, bathroom count, and floor level.
    • Building Density: Number of flats sharing the street address and estimated block growth (2021–2026).
    • Parking Premium Decomposition: Paired delta regression estimating the isolated value of a parking space by building cluster ($54.00/wk median premium across the dataset).
  • What Worked vs What Did Not:
    • What Worked: Histogram-based Gradient Boosting Regressors using L1 loss (Least Absolute Deviation) directly optimized for Median Absolute Error. Incorporating the normalized building_address cluster reduced error by an additional $8.40/wk.
    • What Did Not: Standard OLS Linear Regression ($R^2 = 0.62$, Median Error $89.50$) failed on luxury multi-bedroom penthouses. Unregularized target encoding overfit single-lease boutique blocks.
  • Submission File: submission/rents_oneshot.csv contains exactly 65,777 rows, median rent with car $940.00/wk vs without car $872.00/wk.

Task 5: City of Sydney Valuation Architecture

  • Dataset: Ingested 81,079 bulk sales records from NSW Valuer General and Microburbs transaction history.
  • Model: Repeat-sales index paired with a Hedonic Gradient Boosting regressor with temporal appreciation adjustments.
  • Submission File: submission/valuation_oneshot.csv contains all 181,711 G-NAF address IDs, verified zero NaNs, zero negatives, median valuation $773,041.

Why We Chose These Specific Facts

  1. Underquote Reality Corridor: The single most expensive mistake first-home buyers make is spending $600–$1,000 on strata inspections for homes quoted at $720k that sell for $850k. Flagging the true AVM corridor upfront saves buyers thousands in sunk costs.
  2. Inside The Building Ledger: Suburb medians are too coarse for apartment buyers. What matters is what the identical unit two floors up sold for. By resolving the street address to building clusters, we give buyers insider transparency.
  3. Gross Rental Yield & Cash Flow Reality: Combines Task 4 rental predictions ($940/wk) with mortgage calculations (6.1% P&I + strata) to reveal true monthly holding costs ($4,370/mo outlay vs $4,073/mo income = -$297/mo net flow).

5. Security, Privacy & Data Compliance

MicroTruth strictly complies with the hackathon rules and Australian privacy standards:

  • No Listing Text or Photo Scraping: The extension reads only the property address from the page header or user input to query the API. No images, descriptions, or agent contact details are collected.
  • API Key Security: The team API key is stored server-side in .env and accessed via a local reverse proxy. Zero credentials are committed to GitHub or exposed in clientside DOM.
  • Open Data Licensing: G-NAF addresses, Valuer General bulk sales, and ABS Census data are used strictly within their non-commercial hackathon research terms with full source attribution.
