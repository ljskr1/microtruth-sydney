1. Executive Summary & Market Problem

In the Sydney property market, buyers and tenants experience acute information asymmetry. Real estate portals (Domain.com.au, Realestate.com.au) serve the vendor and listing agent. Price guides are frequently underquoted by 15% to 25% to generate artificial bidding competition, while vital livability hazards—such as designated flood zones, high-voltage power lines, upcoming 20-storey tower development approvals, and social housing density—are completely omitted from the listing.

MicroTruth Sydney unmasks this information asymmetry by placing independent, certified municipal geospatial, automated valuation intelligence (AVM), and true building-level comp ledgers directly beside the listing in real-time.

Task 4: Rent Pricing Architecture

  • Dataset: 112,457 historical leases across City of Sydney. 103,919 training rows, 8,538 held-out validation rows (2026 test set).
  • Model: Histogram-based Gradient Boosting Regressor (L1 Median Loss) with spatial target encodings (suburb median rent, property type rent baseline, bedroom count interaction).
  • Submission File: submission/rents_oneshot.csv (exactly 65,777 rows, median rent with car $940/wk vs without car $872/wk, reflecting a clean $54/wk parking premium).

Task 5: Capital Valuation Architecture

  • Dataset: 81,079 recorded sales transactions paired with repeat-sales appreciation indices.
  • Model: Hedonic GBDT with temporal decay weights and building-level feature tracking.
  • Submission File: submission/valuation_oneshot.csv (exactly 181,711 rows in whole integer dollars, verified zero NaNs or negatives).

5. Data & Architectural Method

MicroTruth strictly adheres to the hackathon privacy contract: "Read only the viewed address, or ask the user to type it. Get your facts from our API after matching the address. Do not collect or copy any listing text or photos."

  • Address Resolution: The content script extracts the property address from the page header and calls GET /v1/geocode/address?address={query} on the Microburbs API to resolve the exact G-NAF identifier.
  • Local SQLite Ledger: Ingested 112,457 leases, 81,079 sales, 6,597 public housing homes, and 213,239 city addresses in WAL mode for sub-2ms query responses.
  • Client Security & Privacy: The Chrome extension communicates with a local proxy server holding the team bearer key. The API key is never exposed to the browser page or committed to git.
